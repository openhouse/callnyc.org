# NYC Council CouncilStat release

The Council described CouncilStat as an application used by many district
offices to track constituent issues. It said the data would be updated daily on
NYC Open Data.

This establishes the 2016 publication commitment. It does not establish when
CouncilStat ended or why a later operational system did or did not inherit its
publication path.
