# Current Council Connect use

An official District 18 job posting published February 24, 2026 instructs a
constituent-services liaison to enter cases into Council Connect and describes
it as a Council-wide constituent-service database.

This is direct evidence of current operational use in one office and of the
Council's own system description. It does not document the exact systemwide
cutover, data model, or public-export history.
