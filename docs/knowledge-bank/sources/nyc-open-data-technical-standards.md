# NYC Open Data Technical Standards Manual

- **Source ID:** `source.open-data-technical-standards`
- **URL:** https://opendata.cityofnewyork.us/wp-content/uploads/NYC_OpenData_TechnicalStandardsManual.pdf
- **Publisher:** NYC Office of Technology and Innovation, Open Data Team
- **Accessed:** 2026-08-13
- **Public projection:** approved for institutional contact and publication-process evidence

## What it establishes

The manual identifies `opendata@oti.nyc.gov` as an Open Data Team address. It describes the Open Data Team as supporting agencies with publication and automation, and states that agencies remain responsible for their datasets, privacy review, and updates.

## Limit

The manual does not assign responsibility for the historical Council dataset’s changed publication status and does not explain why no current Council Connect export was located.
