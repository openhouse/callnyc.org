# What happened at the CouncilStat-to-Council Connect transition?

present-tense export, but it does not yet document the exact cutover or why the
2016 daily publication commitment was not carried forward.

Useful next evidence would include an official retirement notice, procurement
or migration record, Open Data compliance plan, Council Connect data
dictionary, or a statement from the Council and OTI data stewards.
