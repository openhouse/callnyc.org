# Why is no current Council Connect export visible on NYC Open Data?

- **ID:** `question.public-export-transition`
- **Status:** Open
- **Checked:** 2026-08-13

The official dataset is historical, while an official Council posting describes current Council Connect use. Our review found no current Council Connect export on the NYC Open Data Portal and located no official explanation for the change.

“Not found” is the finding. “Does not exist” is not yet established.

Useful next evidence would include an official data-retirement notice, system transition documentation, an Open Data compliance plan, a data dictionary, or a statement from the responsible Council and OTI stewards.
