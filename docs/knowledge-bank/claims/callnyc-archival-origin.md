# CallNYC archival origin

CallNYC is a 2016 independent civic-data project that Jamie Burkart built in a
24-hour sprint from the Council's then-daily, anonymized CouncilStat release.

The Council release and contemporary Politico coverage support the chronology
and build context. They do not make CallNYC an official Council product or
establish formal adoption.
