# Current Council Connect publication gap

Official sources identify Council Connect as a current Council-wide
constituent-service database. NYC Open Data labels Council casework through
early 2025 as historical, and an August 13, 2026 catalog review found no
current Council Connect export comparable in cadence and accessibility to the
daily 311 feed.

## Limits

- No located official source states the exact CouncilStat retirement date,
  Council Connect cutover date, or reason the publication path changed.
- “Not found” is the finding; “does not exist anywhere” is not established.
- The 311 comparison is about public data availability, not equivalent service
  semantics.
