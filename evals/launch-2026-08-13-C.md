# Launch C evaluation record

## Objective

Preserve the original CallNYC interface at the public root while adding only a compact archival, press, and public-action layer in the inherited visual language.

## Deterministic score

| Iteration | Score | Material result |
|---|---:|---|
| Red baseline | 15/100 | Existing safety repairs passed; the contemporary homepage, prefixed routes, and oversized archive boundary violated the launch-C brief. |
| First green | 100/100 | Original root and routes restored; compact header, Politico artifact, and pre-populated email present. |

The executable scorecard is `evals/launch-2026-08-13-C.php`.

## Visual hill climb

Round 1 reviewed 1440px desktop, 820px tablet, and 390px mobile layouts plus the open mobile navigation. It found no horizontal overflow, console errors, page errors, or logo-loading failures. It did identify insufficient contrast in the compact header button, the archive heading, and the inherited coral hero copy.

Round 2 darkened the action and heading within the Materialize red family and changed the hero copy to white. A final two-step darkening of the inherited hero coral (`#ee6e73` to `#ec6c71`) brought the large white copy to 3.02:1 while remaining visually continuous with the original interface. The expanded final check reports 5.62:1 for the email action, 4.98:1 for the archive label, 3.02:1 for the large hero copy, 5.60:1 for the card action at rest, on hover, and on focus, and 5.62:1 for the footer data action at all three widths. The preservation action is 44px high; no viewport overflow, console error, page error, or logo failure was recorded.

## Publication boundary and archive safety

`evals/http-public-boundary.sh` verifies that the root, health endpoint, public Politico media, and local jQuery fallback remain available while dotfiles, environment files, the internal wiki, tests, evals, maintenance scripts, Impeccable review files, and root design documents return `404`. The Docker context also excludes repository metadata, environment files, and internal evidence. The legacy updater returns `403` when `CALLNYC_ARCHIVED` is omitted because archive safety now fails closed.

Browser evidence and machine-readable reports live under `.impeccable/review/launch-c-round-1/` and `.impeccable/review/launch-c-round-2/`; the exact post-polish runtime result is `.impeccable/review/launch-c-final-check/report.json`.
