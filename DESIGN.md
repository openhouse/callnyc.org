# Design System: CallNYC Launch B

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Working Civic Artifact"**

CallNYC Launch B is the preserved 2016 interface acting as its own public argument. The database-backed explorer—not a replacement homepage—owns the visitor's experience: the original logo, coral banner, fixed category rail, district-office ranking cards, photographs, service chips, and Materialize motion and depth remain in command.

The present-day layer is deliberately narrow. A compact white preservation notice precedes the coral banner, using the same Roboto family, material controls, two-pixel corners, reds, teal link language, and modest elevation already present in the artifact. It names the archive boundary, links to current official help and the historical Politico record, and makes a privacy-protective restoration request without creating a second visual identity.

The previous blue, serif, public-record-room successor system and the Open Letter A/B/C comps are superseded and non-governing. They are process history only. New work must begin from the implemented original-Materialize artifact and preserve the compact-extension relationship established by Launch B.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Original 2016 Materialize language, with locally served Roboto and recognizable CallNYC coral.
- A working civic-data interface before extended explanation.
- A fixed white category rail, strong coral banner, elevated photo cards, and compact service chips.
- Present-day stewardship expressed as one concise notice inside the incumbent world.
- Archived, unofficial, historical, and privacy-protective status visible before rankings.

**The Artifact Is the Argument Rule.** Never place a promotional, editorial, or explanatory landing page in front of the explorer.

**The Narrow Extension Rule.** Present-day additions borrow the artifact's palette, type, shapes, density, and interaction grammar; they do not establish a successor brand.

## Colors

CallNYC's color character is direct early-Material civic UI: coral establishes identity and major fields, teal carries ordinary actions and official routes, and neutral white surfaces keep dense data legible.

### Primary

- **CallNYC Coral:** The banner, active archive state, footer, and recognizable historical identity.
- **Advocacy Red:** The compact preservation notice heading, mobile menu control, and strongest restore-the-data action.
- **Deep Advocacy Red:** Emphasis inside the preservation request.

### Secondary

- **Material Teal:** Standard historical controls and the inherited Materialize action language.
- **Official Teal:** The current-Council route and preservation-notice link treatment.
- **Focus Yellow:** A high-visibility focus outline; it also recalls the artifact's original rank-accent vocabulary without becoming decoration.
- **Card Action Orange:** Historical ranking-card action links only.

### Neutral

- **Archive Canvas:** The slightly warm off-white page field.
- **Paper White:** Fixed rail, notice, search field, chips' containers, and card surfaces.
- **Ink:** Default high-emphasis copy.
- **Body Ink:** Paragraphs and preservation copy.
- **Secondary Ink:** Chip and supporting text.
- **Divider:** Preservation and documentary borders.
- **Skip Ink:** The dark skip-link field.

**The Coral Owns Identity Rule.** CallNYC coral governs the banner and archive identity; advocacy red intensifies only the small present-day request.

**The Teal Means Route Rule.** Teal carries ordinary material action and the official-help route. Do not replace it with the obsolete successor blue.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Locally served Roboto with a sans-serif fallback

**Body Font:** Locally served Roboto with a sans-serif fallback

**Label Font:** Locally served Roboto with a sans-serif fallback

**Character:** One pragmatic humanist sans family keeps the archive recognizable and lets weight, scale, image, and material depth carry hierarchy. Light Roboto makes the banner and card titles open; regular and medium weights keep data and navigation compact.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (300, `2.5em`, 1.1): The Call NYC banner title and active historical category.
- **Headline** (300, `2rem`, 1.15): The preservation notice title; it reduces to `1.65rem` on small screens.
- **Title** (300, `24px`, 1.1): District member names over card photography.
- **Body** (400, `1rem`, 1.5): Historical data, card content, footer explanation, and general interface copy.
- **Support** (400, `.96rem`, 1.5): Compact preservation explanation, capped at roughly 75 characters per line.
- **Label** (500, `13px`): Category links, service chips, buttons, and dense navigation.
- **Caption** (500, `.78rem`, `.06em`, uppercase): The Politico artifact label.

The root font responds with the incumbent Materialize steps: `14px` by default, `14.5px` from 992px, and `15px` from 1200px. The extension follows these inherited rem proportions rather than creating an independent fluid type scale.

**The One Roboto World Rule.** Do not introduce serif display faces, Karla, Oswald, or remotely hosted typography into Launch B.

**The Light Title Rule.** Large titles use the 300 weight. Data labels, action copy, and navigation earn medium or bold weight through function, not decorative contrast.

## Layout

At desktop widths, a fixed 240px white category rail occupies the left edge while header, main, and footer receive matching 240px left padding. The rail contains the logo, a raised search field, nested category navigation, and active-state coral. Main content uses the inherited Materialize grid; containers expand to 85% width from 993px, ranking cards remain the primary reading column, and the table of contents provides a compact in-page index.

The preservation notice is the only new top-level composition. It is a compact two-column grid with a flexible copy field and a 10.5rem Politico thumbnail, separated from the original coral banner by a one-pixel rule. Its padding follows the artifact's existing 7.5%/container alignment so it reads as a cap on the live interface, not a standalone page.

At 992px and below, the fixed rail moves off canvas, page left padding disappears, and a 48px red menu control exposes navigation. The notice keeps a narrow 9rem artifact column and reserves top space for that control. At 600px and below, the notice becomes one column, tightens its type and spacing, turns the advocacy button full-width, and presents the Politico image as a compact 5rem thumbnail beside its label. The banner and ranking interface immediately follow.

**The First-Viewport Continuity Rule.** The preservation notice must stay compact enough that visitors see the original coral banner and understand that a working interface follows.

**The 240px Rail Rule.** Desktop extensions align with the fixed rail rather than centering themselves across the full browser canvas.

## Elevation & Depth

Launch B uses the inherited Materialize elevation vocabulary. Cards, the search field, fixed rail, and standard buttons rest at the first material depth; buttons lift on hover. District photographs and title text create additional figure-ground separation inside ranking cards. The compact Politico thumbnail uses a smaller documentary shadow, keeping it legible as a page image without turning it into a successor-system centerpiece.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Material Rest:** `0 2px 5px rgba(0,0,0,.16), 0 2px 10px rgba(0,0,0,.12)` for cards, rail, search, and buttons at rest.
- **Material Hover:** `0 5px 11px rgba(0,0,0,.18), 0 4px 15px rgba(0,0,0,.15)` for actionable material controls on hover.
- **Document Thumbnail:** `0 5px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.18)` for the compact Politico page image.
- **Photo Title Legibility:** the established layered black text shadow behind white card titles over district photographs.

**The Material Depth Rule.** Depth communicates an interactive or physical surface. Preserve the inherited shadows; do not flatten the ranking cards into editorial rows or amplify the notice into a floating panel.

## Shapes

The governing material corner is a tight two-pixel radius across cards, buttons, the search field, and the mobile navigation control. Service chips are functional capsules with a 16px radius and 32px height. Rank markers are small circular badges ringed in black over card photography. Photography clips only to the two top card corners; the card body and action field complete the rectangular silhouette.

**The Two-Pixel Material Rule.** New controls inside the artifact use the established two-pixel material corner.

**The Capsule Is Metadata Rule.** Rounded capsules belong to service chips; do not turn advocacy actions, notices, or arbitrary labels into pills.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Shape:** Compact material rectangle with a two-pixel radius and uppercase Roboto label.
- **Advocacy Primary:** Deep red field, white text, 40px height, and inherited horizontal padding; used once in the preservation notice.
- **Standard Historical:** Teal field, white text, 36px height, and inherited material shadow.
- **Hover / Focus:** Hover lifts to the material-hover shadow. Keyboard focus uses a three-pixel yellow outline with a three-pixel offset.
- **Mobile:** The preservation action fills the notice width below 601px.

### Service Chips

- **Style:** 32px-high pale-gray capsules with secondary ink, 13px medium Roboto, and 12px horizontal padding.
- **Purpose:** Historical top-service links within ranking cards. They may wrap, but their text remains clipped safely within the capsule.

### Ranking Cards

- **Corner Style:** Two-pixel material card with only the top corners clipping its photograph.
- **Background:** White card surface over the archive canvas.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Material Rest at rest.
- **Image:** Full-width district photograph with the member and district title overlaid in white.
- **Internal Padding:** 20px for content and action regions.
- **Action:** Uppercase historical action link separated by a subtle top rule.

### Archive Search

- **Style:** A 45px white field inside a raised card wrapper, positioned below the fixed-rail logo.
- **Icon:** A 22px authored SVG search mark in CallNYC coral; never an external icon font.
- **Focus:** The input participates in the global three-pixel yellow focus treatment.

### Fixed Category Rail

The 240px white rail is both navigation and the desktop identity frame. Links use dense 44px rows; bold category headers expand nested service lists; the selected subcategory reverses to white on coral. Below 993px it becomes an off-canvas surface opened by the 48px red menu control.

### Preservation Notice

The notice is a compact white two-column stewardship cap. A dark Materialize red headline and short Roboto paragraphs share space with one restore-data button, one official-help link, and a small Politico page thumbnail. Its job is to prevent misreading and offer action without delaying entry into the artifact.

### Rank Marker

The rank marker is a small white circle with a black three-pixel ring placed over the card photograph. It is an inherited data cue, not a general badge pattern.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** preserve the original CallNYC logo, coral banner, Roboto type, fixed rail, ranking cards, photographs, chips, and material depth.
- **Do** keep the preservation notice immediately above the banner and visually subordinate to the explorer.
- **Do** state archived, unofficial, and historical status before any ranking appears.
- **Do** pair the restore-data request with explicit privacy protection and a route to the current official Council directory.
- **Do** treat the Politico page as attributed historical evidence, not endorsement.
- **Do** retain the 992px rail transition, 600px stacked notice, 48px mobile menu target, full-width mobile action, and visible focus outline.
- **Do** use authored SVG controls and locally served assets.

### Don't:

- **Don't** restore the blue/serif public-record-room successor system or use Open Letter A/B/C comps as governing references.
- **Don't** place an open letter, hero campaign, evidence docket, or explanatory landing page before the explorer.
- **Don't** present historical rankings, officeholders, categories, or annualized values as current.
- **Don't** ask for raw or identifiable constituent case files.
- **Don't** replace Materialize coral and teal with Broadway blue, ochre, editorial ink/paper tokens, or a separate contemporary palette.
- **Don't** introduce serif display type, Karla, Oswald, gradients, glass, faux-government motifs, or generic civic seals.
- **Don't** flatten ranking cards, convert them into an editorial list, or promote the compact notice into a second product surface.
