# CallNYC.org launch C

## Direction contract

- **Structure:** The original 2016 interface is the public root; one compact preservation header precedes it.
- **Approved composition:** The preserved Materialize implementation, checked against two predecessor-archive 2016 screenshots documented in the knowledge bank.
- **Thesis:** Experience the original civic-data affordances immediately; understand their historical boundary without leaving the page.
- **Audience priority:** A civic hiring reader can use the artifact in the first viewport; residents are never directed to obsolete phone numbers.
- **Own-world rule:** Preserve CallNYC's original Roboto, coral, fixed sidebar, categories, ranking cards, and responsive drawer.
- **Evidence rule:** Historical data, one current office's documented Council Connect use, and the unexplained absence of a located current public export remain separate claims with separate sources.
- **Artifact rule:** The real March 14, 2016 Politico New York page is evidence of contemporary reception, not an endorsement.
- **Finish rule:** Desktop, tablet, mobile, and open-drawer screenshots, the single Impeccable detector pass, a fresh finish review, and final documentation are part of the artifact.

## Required inventory

1. Original CallNYC interface at `/`, with original direct category paths.
2. Compact archived/unofficial and transparent authorship notice.
3. Politico thumbnail with meaningful alt text and archived PDF link.
4. Pre-populated privacy-protective email to NYC Council and NYC Open Data.
5. Current official Council lookup in place of historical phone actions.
6. Responsive drawer, keyboard focus, user zoom, and self-hosted presentation assets.
7. Project-internal typed knowledge graph whose public projection is separately governed.
8. No additional public content pages.
