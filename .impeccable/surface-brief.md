## Product and surface truth

- The public root is one database-backed artifact. Root paths and `/archive/2016` aliases resolve through the same historical explorer.
- The interface is archived and unofficial. Its 2016 data, rankings, officeholders, categories, and annualized values are historical and do not describe current services or Council members.
- The preservation notice is a small extension, not a header for a second site. It must not displace the explorer from the first viewport.
- The primary present-day action asks NYC Council and NYC OTI data teams to restore constituent-services publishing with privacy protections.
- The official-help action routes visitors to the current NYC Council district directory.
- The archived Politico New York page documents contemporary coverage. It is evidence, not endorsement.
- Source-bounded public wording, archived/unofficial status, constituent privacy, and human publication/deployment authorization remain separate safeguards.
